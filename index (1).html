Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you start.

How does my club get started with PGO?

Request a demo to explore the platform. To accept bookings, your club must be approved, with active courts, schedules, slot lengths and prices configured.

How do players book?

Players open PGO, choose a club, date and duration, then select an available slot in the timetable to continue booking.

How are payments handled?

Players follow the payment instructions and methods offered by the club. This landing page does not process bank payments.
